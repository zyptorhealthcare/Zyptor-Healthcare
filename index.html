Getting Started

Fitted in three steps.

Takes under a minute once you know your size — see the size guide below.

01

Wrap & Fasten

Position the belt over your lower back, pull the elastic ends around your waist, and fasten for a snug base fit.

02

Cross-Lock the Straps

Cross the elastic straps in front for double-lock support, adjusting tension until the back panel sits firmly in place.

03

Check the Fit

You should feel firm, even pressure with no pinching — snug enough to support, loose enough to breathe in.
